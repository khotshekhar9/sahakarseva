Cooperative Architecture

Built for Dignity, Fair Pay & Statutory Trust

Unlike exploitative venture aggregators charging 25–35% commissions, SahakarSeva operates as a democratic digital public infrastructure governed by registered labour societies.

Direct Cooperative Hiring

Eliminate commercial middlemen. Citizen service requests dispatch straight to primary ward-level labour cooperatives, guaranteeing local talent retention and community accountability.

Statutory 88/8/4 Wage Split

88% goes directly into the worker's pocket, 8% sustains primary cooperative society operations, and 4% is automatically reserved in the Suraksha Nidhi worker welfare trust fund.

Suraksha Nidhi Insurance

Automated 4% micro-cess funding PMJJBY life cover, PMSBY accidental disability coverage (₹2,00,000 policy), and emergency medical assistance with zero deduction from base wages.

QR-Verified Site Attendance

Geo-fenced, cryptographic QR check-ins and check-outs protect housing societies and residential complexes against unverified contractor substitution and attendance fraud.

Real-Time WebSocket Dispatch

Sub-second bidirectional socket notifications stream job postings, arrival updates, and milestone completions simultaneously across citizen, worker, and society supervisor portals.

Apex Federation Governance

Statutory conciliation, quality audits, and dispute mediation backed by the State Cooperative Labour Federation under statutory bylaws and transparent digital audit trails.
